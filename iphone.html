73 iPhone

Radio apps for iPhone that do the same job as apps on the Android list. If an Android app has no real iPhone match, it isn't listed here.

I really suggest you buy a dedicated Android phone for radio. You can find them for as little as $30 USD. Look up the Motorola Moto G Play, sold at Walmart.

It doesn't need a service plan or any monthly bill. We're simply using it like a small computer. Set it up on Wi-Fi with no SIM card, download your apps at home, and use them on the air. Buy one marked Prepaid, not Postpaid: postpaid phones come tied to a monthly plan.

Many of the radio apps on this site only exist on Android, so an Android phone gets you the whole list. It also keeps your everyday phone free while you're on the air.

iPhone limits to know about:

  • iOS doesn't let apps open USB serial ports, so you won't get CAT control on most radios.
  • A Digirig Lite is required for a lot of these apps to connect to your radio.
